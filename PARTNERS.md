# AegisGate Design Partner Program

AegisGate Security is looking for 3-5 security teams to help validate the platform in production. This is a structured feedback program — not a sales funnel.

## What Partners Get

- **Free Enterprise tier access** for the duration of the partnership
- **Direct roadmap input** — your use cases inform what we build
- **Priority support** — direct access to the founder
- **Early access** to new features before public release
- **Co-marketing option** (with explicit consent only)

## What We Ask For

- **Production deployment** against real AI traffic
- **Monthly 30-min feedback call**
- **Honest feedback** — bugs, friction, feature requests

## What We Don't Ask For

- No long-term contract
- No exclusivity
- No public reference without explicit written consent
- No minimum deployment size

## Who We're Looking For

- Security teams evaluating AI tools
- Compliance teams in regulated industries (healthcare, finance, government, SLED)
- DevOps/Platform teams deploying AI infrastructure
- AI startups building agent-based products (MCP, A2A, ACP, ANP)

## Why Trust Us

- **[CSA STAR Registry listed](https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/star/registry/aegisgate-security)** (Level 1)
- **[CSA Startup Showcase featured](https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/csa-startup-showcase/registry/?modal=aegisgate-security-llc)**
- **First public [AICM crosswalk](docs/aicm/)** — 247 controls mapped to source code
- **Apache 2.0 open-source** — audit every line before deployment
- **Self-hosted, air-gapped capable** — your data never leaves your infrastructure

## Program Constraints

- Maximum 5 partners (solo founder)
- No custom feature guarantees
- Best-effort support (1 business day response)
- Month-to-month, cancel anytime
